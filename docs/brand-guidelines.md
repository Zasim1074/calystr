# CALYSTR Brand Guidelines

## Brand foundation

- Public name: **Calystr Studio**
- Wordmark: **CALYSTR**
- Pronunciation: **Cal-iss-tr**
- Tagline: **Engineering beyond expectations.**
- Character: sharp, modern, technical, precise and premium.

## Color system

The dark canvas is a neutral stage. Within visible brand color, use the palette in a **6:3:1 ratio**, led by red:

| Role | Color | Hex | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Primary | Signal Red | `#F04432` | 60% | Primary actions, signature surfaces, logo cut |
| Secondary | Electric Blue | `#315CFF` | 30% | Structural emphasis and digital depth |
| Contrast | Warm White | `#F2F0EA` | 10% | Typography, clarity and breathing room |
| Canvas | Midnight | `#07080B` | Neutral | Main background |
| Surface | Deep Navy | `#0E1118` | Neutral | Cards and raised surfaces |

## Logo

The mark is a geometric open C with two red precision cuts. Use the full horizontal logo in navigation and the symbol alone for favicons or compact placements. Keep clear space equal to one symbol width and do not add gradients, shadows, rotation or alternate colors to the mark.

## Typography and voice

Use lightweight editorial headlines, compact technical labels and highly readable body copy. Write with confidence and specificity. Avoid inflated claims, excessive jargon and generic agency language.
